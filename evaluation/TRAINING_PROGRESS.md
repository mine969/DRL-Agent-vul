# 📈 Training Progress & Evaluation Log

This document tracks the performance of the AI security agent throughout the training process.
Evaluations are performed automatically every 40 episodes.

## 📊 Metrics Legend

- **Episode**: Training episode number
- **Avg Reward**: Average reward over evaluation episodes
- **Vulns Found**: Number of vulnerabilities successfully exploited
- **Success Rate**: Percentage of successful attacks
- **Epsilon**: Exploration rate at time of evaluation
