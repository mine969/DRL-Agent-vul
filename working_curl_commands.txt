📝 URL ENCODING EXPLANATION
================================================================================

Original payload: ' AND 1=1--
URL encoded:      %27%20AND%201%3D1--

Character mapping:
' (single quote) = %27
  (space)        = %20
= (equals)       = %3D
- (dash)         = %2D (not needed for --)
